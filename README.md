This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Home Credit loan applicants. It helps financial institutions evaluate customer demographics, credit risk, previous loan applications, payment behavior, and default trends through interactive dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Business Problem

Financial institutions process thousands of loan applications every day. Identifying customers with a high probability of default is essential for reducing financial risk, improving lending decisions, and optimizing credit approval strategies. This dashboard provides decision-makers with a complete view of customer profiles and loan performance to support data-driven credit risk management.

## Key Insights

- The overall default rate is approximately **8%**, indicating a relatively small but significant high-risk customer segment.
- Male applicants show a higher default rate than female applicants.
- Lower-income customers are more likely to default on loans.
- Cash loans represent the largest share of issued credit.
- Most previous loan applications were approved, while a smaller percentage were refused.
- Consumer credit accounts represent the majority of bureau records.
- Outstanding debt is concentrated within consumer credit products.
- Customer demographics such as occupation, education, and family status have a noticeable impact on credit performance.
